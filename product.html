Care ritual

  1. Insert cedar shoe trees after every wear.
  2. Brush with a horsehair brush before polishing.
  3. Condition the leather every 6–8 wears.
  4. Polish with a matching wax polish.
  5. Rotate pairs: give them 24h rest between wears.
