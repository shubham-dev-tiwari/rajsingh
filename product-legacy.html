  1. 01

    Pattern & cutting

    Closing & stitching

Common Questions

How long will my order take to arrive?

Since every pair of Raj Singh shoes is handcrafted to order by our master artisans, it takes 7 business days to polish, finish, and deliver them directly to your door.

How do I know I'm ordering the correct size?

We follow standard UK/India sizing. If you're in between sizes, we recommend selecting your usual size. Check our detailed Sizing Chart on the product page or message us on WhatsApp for personalized size assistance.

What makes Raj Singh shoes more durable?

We use premium Italian Marble Patina leather for the upper, paired with our signature handmade leather sole featuring an embedded anti-skid rubber grip. This ensures both luxury comfort and long-lasting durability.

What if the shoes don't fit or I want to return them?

We offer a hassle-free 15-day return and exchange policy. Simply message our team on WhatsApp at +91 87555 32123, and we will arrange a pickup. Refunds are credited directly to your bank account.
